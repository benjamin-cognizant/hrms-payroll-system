Employee Lifecycle & Payroll Management System

Comprehensive Human Resources & Workforce Management

1. System Overview

The Employee Lifecycle & Payroll Management System manages HR operations end to end. Built using a robust MVC architecture, this platform seamlessly handles everything from initial recruitment and candidate onboarding to daily attendance, complex payroll processing, and yearly performance appraisals.

Detailed Module Architecture

1. Recruitment & Candidate Onboarding

Manages job requisitions, candidate selection, and onboarding workflows. It tracks applicants through applied, interviewing, offered, and hired stages.

2. Employee Master & Organizational Hierarchy

Maintains core employee master data, department mapping, and reporting relationships to build accurate organizational charts.

3. Attendance, Leave & Timesheet Management

Records daily attendance, processes employee leave requests, applies attendance policies, and tracks detailed timesheets.

4. Payroll Computation & Compliance Filing

Calculates monthly net salaries by applying salary structures and computing statutory deductions (PF, ESI, TDS) to generate compliance reports and payslips.

5. Performance Appraisal & Goal Tracking

Manages goal setting and entire performance review cycles. It facilitates self-reviews, manager calibrations, and publishes final overall ratings based on achieved goals.
